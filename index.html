The case事件

Osaka, 25 January 1925. Ren Kagami, 26, is found dead in his room at home. Confirmed suicide, but his death is tied to several other murders. All the police found were seven diaries, each in different handwriting, each signed with a different day of the week. Ren shared his body with seven personalities. They took turns, one a day, and never met. They spoke only through notes, and they promised never to read each other's diaries.

Tonight, you will.
